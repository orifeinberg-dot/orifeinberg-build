# Product brief: SafeNest (a made-up example, not a real company)

**Product:** SafeNest is a mobile app that helps families stay safe from scam messages and weak passwords. It has a shared family password vault, a "Check this message" tool (paste a text or email and get a plain-words answer about whether it looks like a scam), and an alert when a family member's email shows up in a known data leak.

**Audience:** Adults 30 to 65 who help family members with their phones, and parents of teenagers. They are not technical. They worry about scam texts and reused passwords, but they do not want to read a manual.

**Main benefit:** One simple place where a whole family can check suspicious messages and keep passwords in order, without needing to be a tech expert.

**Offer:** Free for 14 days, then $4.99 per month. Cancel any time.

**Tone:** Warm, calm, a little funny. Like a friend giving a tip. Never scary, never preachy.

**Platform and format:** Vertical video (9:16), 15 to 20 seconds, for Facebook and Instagram Reels and Stories.

**Must NOT claim:**
- That SafeNest gives 100% protection, or guarantees anything
- That the viewer's phone, account or data "is hacked" or "is being watched"
- Any before-and-after results or numbers we cannot prove
- That the speaker is a real customer or a real expert
- That a government, bank, police force or any named company endorses or works with SafeNest
- Any fake statistics, awards, or "as seen on" claims
- That SafeNest stops all scams, or replaces a bank's own security
